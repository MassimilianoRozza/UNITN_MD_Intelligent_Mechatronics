**Digital Manufacturing** is the evolution of manufacturing, where an **integrated** approach is centered around a computer **system.** It fits in the framework of Industry 4.0, where many cyber physical systems work together thanks to interoperable and interconnected open protocols acting on the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT).

The essence of Digital Manufacturing is the **full digitalization** of process data and the possible digitalization of machines, by the development of **digital twins**.
