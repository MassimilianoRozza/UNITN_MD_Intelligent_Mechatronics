Relatore: Antonio Priore
# High Integrity
system whose failure could cause serous harm or loss, designed and assured so it behaves dependably within defined conditions, including when faults occur.
# Functionnal safety
absence of unreasonavle risl due to hazards coused by malfunctioning behavior of the electrical and electronic systems
# Hazard and Risk management
- identify hazards
- consequence analisys
- evaluate probability/rate
- derive safety margins
# Defence-in-depth
multi-layered strategy that spans across technologies, system hierarchies and processes.
to try to ensure safety:
- many layers and as few holes as possible.
# Failure analysis methods
- Failure Modes and Effects Analysis
- Failure modes, Effects and Diagnostics Analysis
- Fault Tree Analysis:
	- top-down analysis, start from the effects, than you try to define the events that could cause the top event.
	- can be qualitative just to gives ideas of the failure possibilities.
	- can be quantitative by giving a probability
- common cause failure/dependent failure analysis: tries to defined the single common origin of most of most of the problems
- system theoretic process analysis: look the system and its behavoiour not the components.
# Functional Safety
Standards are not Recipes, not to follow blindly but try always to improve
