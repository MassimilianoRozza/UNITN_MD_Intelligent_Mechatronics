System with many DOF can be seen as a **linear combination** of more 1-DOF systems.
## Spring Element
- Element that stores elastic potential energy.
- Usually rappresented as massless
![[Spring element analysis.webp]]
The status of a spring is described by:
- $x_1$ and $x_2$ are the displacements from the unperturbed configuration
- $F_{1}$ and $F_2$ external forces
- $G$ center of mass (used only in this analysis befoure assuming the objec massless);
- $x_G$ center of mass displacement
The differential equation that rules the motion of the spring is:
$$
\begin{matrix}
\sum F_{i}=m \ddot{x}_{g} \\
F_{2}-F_{1} = m \ddot{x}_{g} \\
\text{If } m=0 \text{:    } F_{2} - F_{1}=0 \rightarrow F_{1} = F_{2} = F
\end{matrix}
$$
Therefore an ideal spring does not accept differential forces at the two ends.
